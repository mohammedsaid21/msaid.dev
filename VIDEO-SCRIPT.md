# Portfolio Video Script (~2 min, face on camera)

A 2-minute face-on-camera video is the single biggest trust asset you can add —
it shows competence + communication + personality in 120 seconds.

## What to SHOW (setup)
- **Audio first** — a cheap mic close to you beats a far laptop mic. Bad audio kills trust instantly.
- **Light your face** — face a window; never put a bright window behind you.
- **Look at the lens**, not your screen — that's eye contact.
- **Clean, simple background.** Stand up while recording (more energy).
- **Conversational, not a formal read** — use bullet points, not a script. Stumbles are fine; authenticity > polish.
- **Optional:** a 5–10s screen recording of a real project during the proof part, and **captions on** (many watch muted).

## What to SAY — 2-min structure (~260 words)

**0:00–0:15 · Hook (the pain, for THEM)**
> "Hi, I'm Mohammed Said — a full-stack product engineer. If you've built an app with Claude, Lovable, or v0… and now you're stuck — it half-works, the code's a mess, you can't ship it — that's exactly what I fix."

**0:15–0:45 · Proof (one real result)**
> "I've shipped 10+ products. One — an e-learning platform — now serves over 15,000 students. I take things from rough prototype to real, production software people actually rely on."

**0:45–1:25 · Why you (kill their top fears)**
> "Most founders get stuck after the AI build because it's not production-ready. I come in, clean it up, fix what's broken, and ship it — fast. I communicate clearly, I hit deadlines, and I don't disappear."

**1:25–1:45 · Low-pressure CTA**
> "If that's your situation, book a free 30-minute call — no obligation. Tell me what you're stuck on, and I'll honestly tell you whether I can help."

**1:45–2:00 · Warm close**
> "I'm Mohammed — let's ship your product. Talk soon."

## Bullet script to glance at (don't read word-for-word)
- I'm Mohammed — full-stack product engineer
- Built your app with Claude / Lovable / v0 and now stuck? That's what I fix
- Shipped 10+ products; one has 15,000+ users
- I turn messy prototypes into real, production software
- Clear communication, deadlines met, no ghosting
- Book a free 30-min call — I'll tell you honestly if I can help

## The 3 rules that matter most
1. **First 5 seconds** — energy + the specific pain; that's what stops the scroll.
2. **Sound human, not salesy** — confidence, not hype.
3. **End with an easy yes** — the free call + "I'll be honest if I can help."
