# Conversion Playbook — How a client decides you're "the perfect person"

> The goal: turn a cold visitor from *"nice site"* into *"this is exactly who I need to hire."*

## The core truth
Design + copy get you to **"looks capable."**
**Trust + proof** get you to **"I'll contact them."**
That trust/proof layer is the gap you're still in.

## The 4 things a client decides on (in this order)
1. **Fit** — "they solve *my exact* problem." Specificity beats "I do everything."
2. **Proof** — "they've solved this before, with real results."
3. **Trust** — "they're real, reliable, low-risk to hire."
4. **Clarity** — the site itself signals competence (premium, fast, clear).

## What actually creates the "perfect person" feeling (ranked)
1. **A real testimonial** — one genuine quote, with name + role, from a real client. Out-converts every animation on the site. Right now a stranger has zero proof others trusted you.
2. **A human face (photo or video)** — people hire *people*. The video you plan to record is gold — it shows how you communicate and think. Builds more trust in 60 seconds than any text. **Prioritize the video.**
3. **Proof that matches their situation** — your best hook is *"I finish AI-built apps."* Strongest proof = a tiny before/after, e.g. *"Took a messy Lovable prototype → shipped a production app in 3 weeks."* A Claude/Lovable client instantly thinks "that's me."
4. **Risk reducers** — free discovery call (done) + a clear process + a promise like *"replies within 24h."* Lower the risk of contacting a stranger.

## The one-sentence answer
The best way to make a client see you're perfect isn't more sections — it's:
**a real testimonial + your face/video + one proof story that mirrors their exact problem.**
That trio flips "capable" into "I'm contacting this person today."
